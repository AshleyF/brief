# 04 JUL 2024 Scopes

Back once again, after another year. What a long-term project this has been.

I have an idea to play with. The current machine state includes a stack, a continuation, and a dictionary. I'm thinking of making a _chain_ of dictionaries, searched in sequence. Opening a quotation (`[`) will append a new dictionary. Word definitions _within_ the quotation will be added to this, and this dictionary will be attached as an environment to the quotation and potentially to a definition within the parent dictionary. This way, sub-definitions can have their own namespaces and can temporarily shadow outer words.
